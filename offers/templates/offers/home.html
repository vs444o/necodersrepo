What is HelpNow?

HelpNow is a platform that connects people who need help with people who can provide it.

Maybe your grandmother needs her house cleaned. Maybe someone needs groceries carried. Maybe someone just needs a hand and can't afford to pay — that's what the volunteer option is for.

Anyone can post a need. Anyone can step up and help.
